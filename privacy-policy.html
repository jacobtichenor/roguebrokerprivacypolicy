Privacy Policy — Rogue Broker

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Rogue Broker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts to create, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

It can optionally send anonymous balance data — how a run went — but only if you switch that on yourself. See below.

What the app stores

Everything the game needs is written to a file inside the app's own storage on your device: your Data Cores, unlocked captains and ranks, permanent upgrades, achievements, collection progress, recent run history, and settings. It is a plain save file. Deleting the app deletes it.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your profile is synchronised across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. That data belongs to your Apple Account. The developer cannot read it, cannot access it, and receives no copy of it.

Sync is optional and automatic. With no internet connection — or signed out of iCloud — the game works exactly as it does offline, saving only to the device, and syncs by itself later if a connection becomes available.

Balance data (off unless you turn it on)

Rogue Broker has a Share balance data switch in Settings. It is off by default and nothing is sent, or even recorded on your device, unless you turn it on.

With it on, a record is sent when a run ends containing only: your net worth at the close of each trading day, the quota each sector demanded and what you were worth when it collected, which relics, devices and ship refits you bought and when, any margin calls along with the size and side of the position that caused each one, which captain and rank you played, and how far the run got. It is used for one thing — tuning how hard the game is.

It does not include your name, email, Apple ID, location, device model, any advertising identifier, or anything you typed. There is no account and no third-party analytics service.

Records go to the app's own iCloud database. Apple stamps each one with an identifier it assigns per app, which cannot be resolved to your Apple ID or name — but it is stable, so several runs from one device are visibly the same device. That is pseudonymous rather than strictly anonymous, and it is described here rather than glossed over.

Turning the switch off stops it immediately and discards anything not yet sent. Erasing your profile does the same.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Daily Challenge scores and achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Declining Game Center has no effect on the game other than hiding the leaderboard.

What the app never does

  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers (no IDFA)
  • No crash reporting and no third-party analytics service
  • No usage tracking beyond the opt-in balance data described below
  • No third-party SDKs or libraries
  • No account creation, email address, or password
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or health data
  • No data is ever sold or shared with anyone

Children

Rogue Broker does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date above revised. Any change that would involve collecting data would ship with a new app version and be described here first.
