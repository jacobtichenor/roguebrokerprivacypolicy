Privacy Policy — Rogue Broker

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Rogue Broker does not collect or transmit any personal information on its own. There are no accounts to create, no advertising, no analytics service and no third-party SDKs of any kind. The game never sends anything in the background.

There are two places where you can choose to send something to the developer, and both work the same way: the app prepares a draft in your own email app, you read it, you edit or delete anything you would rather not send, and you press send. Nothing leaves your device unless you do. Because these are ordinary emails, the address you send from is visible to the developer. Both are described under Email you choose to send.

What the app stores

Everything the game needs is written to a file inside the app's own storage on your device: your Data Cores, unlocked captains and ranks, permanent upgrades, achievements, collection progress, recent run history, and settings. It is a plain save file. Deleting the app deletes it.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your profile is synchronised across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. That data belongs to your Apple Account. The developer cannot read it, cannot access it, and receives no copy of it.

Sync is optional and automatic. With no internet connection or signed out of iCloud the game works exactly as it does offline, saving only to the device, and syncs by itself later if a connection becomes available.

Run data (off unless you turn it on)

Rogue Broker has a Share run data switch in Settings. It is off by default and nothing is recorded unless you turn it on.

With it on, the game keeps a record on your device when a run ends, containing only: your net worth at the close of each trading day, the quota each sector demanded and what you were worth when it collected, which relics, devices and ship refits you bought and when, any margin calls along with the size and side of the position that caused each one, which captain and rank you played, and how far the run got. It is used for one thing — tuning how hard the game is.

It does not include your name, email address, Apple ID, location, device model, any advertising identifier, or anything you typed.

This record stays on your device. The game does not upload it. Settings shows how many runs are stored and gives you three things to do with them: SEND, which prepares an email to the developer with the file attached; EXPORT, which hands the file to the iOS share sheet so you can save it or send it wherever you like; and CLEAR, which deletes it.

Turning the switch off deletes anything already recorded. Erasing your profile does the same.

Email you choose to send

Two features in the app prepare an email for you. In both cases the app only opens a draft — it cannot send mail by itself, you can read and edit every part of it first, and cancelling sends nothing.

Reporting a bug. Settings offers Report a bug. The draft contains what you wrote, plus details of the run you were playing so the problem can be reproduced: the run seed, captain, rank, sector and day, net worth, the quota due, which relics you held, and the app and iOS version. All of it is shown on screen before you send. This is available whether or not Share run data is switched on.

Sending run data. If Share run data is on, the SEND button attaches the stored run file described above to a draft addressed to the developer.

In both cases the email carries your own email address, because that is how email works. It is used only to read what you sent and to reply to you. It is not added to any mailing list, not shared with anyone, and not used for anything else.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Daily Challenge scores and achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Declining Game Center has no effect on the game other than hiding the leaderboard.

What the app never does

  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers (no IDFA)
  • No crash reporting and no third-party analytics service
  • No usage tracking beyond the opt-in run data described above
  • No third-party SDKs or libraries
  • No account to create, no password, and no email address requested or stored by the app
  • No background uploads — nothing is transmitted to the developer unless you send it
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or health data
  • No data is ever sold or shared with anyone

Children

Rogue Broker does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. The game collects nothing on its own, and the only information the developer ever receives is what someone chooses to put in an email and send.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date above revised. Any change that would involve collecting data would ship with a new app version and be described here first.
